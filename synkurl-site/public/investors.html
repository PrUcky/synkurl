Synkurl keeps a family's photos and videos at home, on a small device, and opens them on their phones wherever they are.

The family problem

Family photos live on phones. Phones fill up, so people delete memories to make space. Phones get lost, broken or replaced, and years of photos can go with them. And many families would simply rather keep their memories at home.

Why now

Almost every family moment is now a photo or a video on someone's phone. Families are asking simple questions: where are our photos, and will they still be there when this phone is gone? We want Synkurl to be the plain answer: at home.
