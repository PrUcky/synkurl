Privacy notice

This notice covers synkurl.com: the waitlist, and how we count visits. Updated 10 October 2026. Version 2026-10-10.2.

In short: we keep only what we need to tell you about early access. We never sell it. You can have it deleted any time.

Who we are

Synkurl is a new product for families. In this notice, "we" means Synkurl. You can reach us at synkurl@synkurl.com.

What we collect when you join the waitlist

  • Your email address.
  • Your phone type, your city and how many people are in your family.
  • That you told us you're 18 or older.
  • The language of the page you used, when you joined and confirmed, when we last emailed you, and which version of this notice you agreed to.

We don't ask for your name or phone number.

Why we collect it

Only to tell you about early access to Synkurl, and to plan which phones and places to support first. We don't sell your details or use them for advertising.

Confirming your email

When you join, we send one email to check the address is really yours. It has two links: one to confirm, and one ("Not me: remove my email") that deletes the sign-up straight away. The links work for 48 hours. If a sign-up isn't confirmed within 7 days, we delete it automatically.

If our email system isn't working when you join, we keep your sign-up without asking you to confirm, and use it only as this notice describes.

You must be 18 or older

The waitlist is for adults. If you're under 18, please don't join. If we learn that someone under 18 has joined, we delete their details.

The bot check

To stop spam, our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser before a form is sent. Your connection's IP address is used to limit how many times a form can be sent in a minute. We don't store it.

Counting visits, without cookies

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't follow you around the web. It tells us things like which pages were opened and roughly from which country, not who you are. The site has no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies.

Who helps us (service providers)

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, stores the waitlist, and runs the bot check and visit counts.
  • Resend sends our emails, like the confirmation email.
  • Google Workspace holds our inbox, so it holds any email you send to synkurl@synkurl.com.

They handle your details only to provide these services to us. Their computers may be outside India.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-ups that aren't confirmed: up to 7 days.
  • The waitlist: until early access is over, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can withdraw your consent, see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted at any time. Use the "Not me: remove my email" link in our email, or write to synkurl@synkurl.com from the address you used. We'll do it and reply to confirm.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll change the date and version at the top.
