Privacy notice

This notice covers the Synkurl waitlist on synkurl.com. Updated 10 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your phone type, your city and how many people are in your family.
  • The date you joined and which version of this notice you agreed to.

We don't ask for your name or phone number, and the site has no advertising or tracking scripts. To stop spam sign-ups, the waitlist page uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser before the form is sent.

Why we collect it

Only to tell you about early access to Synkurl, and to plan which phones and cities to support first. We don't sell your details or share them for advertising.

Where it's kept

In a database run by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Only the Synkurl team can read it.

How long we keep it

Until early access is over, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can withdraw your consent, see what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted at any time. Email hello@synkurl.com from the address you signed up with, and we'll do it and reply to confirm.
