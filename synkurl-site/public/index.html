In development · Android first

Your family's photos, kept at home.

Synkurl is building a small home device and a phone app. The aim is simple: automatically save a copy of your photos and videos at home, and let you view them on your phone when you're away.

Join the waitlist See how it will work

What we are building

A copy at home. Easy to find on your phone.

  • Save a copy automatically

    We are working on copying new photos and videos to your home device. A backup means an extra copy, not moving the only copy off your phone.

  • Keep that copy at home

    The device is designed to store your photos and videos in your home.

  • View photos when you are away

    We are building access through the phone app. Your home device and phone will need an internet connection for this.

  1. Connect the device

    Give it power and a connection to your home internet.

  2. Connect your phone

    Install the app and follow the setup steps. We are starting with Android.

  3. Check your saved copies

    See which photos have finished copying. Getting saved photos back onto a new phone is also part of the plan.

A few things to know.

Can I get one now?

We are building and testing the first version. You can join the waitlist to hear when early access opens.

Does a backup free space on my phone?

Making a copy does not free space by itself. We have not confirmed how removing photos from a phone will work.

How much will it store or cost?

Storage options and price have not been announced. We will share more before early access.
