What it does

One small device. Every family photo, safe at home.

  • Backs up by itself

    New photos and videos leave your phone for home without anyone having to remember.

  • Lives in your home

    Your memories sit on a small device in your own home, not in a stranger's data centre.

  • Opens from anywhere

    On Wi-Fi or mobile data, at home or travelling, the whole family album is a tap away.

  1. Plug it in at home

    Connect the Synkurl device to power and your home internet.

  2. Pair your family's phones

    Install the app and pair each phone with the device once.

  3. Let it run

    New photos go home on their own. Get a new phone, and everything comes back to it.
