For families

Your photos should not depend on one phone. Here is the experience we are building for families, starting with Android.

The phone that's always full

Your phone tells you it's full almost every week. It's mostly the kids: the school function, three birthdays, a hundred photos of the same puppy. Every time, you sit and decide which memories to delete.

What we are building

We are working on automatically saving an extra copy at home. Copying does not free phone space by itself. We will explain how phone storage and deleting photos will work before early access.

The new phone that started empty

Papa finally got a new phone for Diwali. It's fast, and the camera is lovely. But years of photos stayed on the old one, which is now in a drawer with a cracked screen.

What we are building

The aim is to let you copy photos saved on the home device to a replacement phone. This recovery flow is still being developed and tested.

Lost on the local train

One crowded evening on the train, the phone was gone. A phone can be replaced. The video of your daughter's first steps can't.

What we are building

A finished copy on the home device would still exist if the phone was lost. Photos that had not finished copying would not be covered.

Our photos, in our own home

Some families simply feel better knowing where their memories are. Not on a computer somewhere they'll never see, but at home, like the old albums in the almirah.

What we are building

The planned photo storage is a device in your home. We are also working out how families should keep a second copy in case that storage fails.

The Diwali photos, far from home

At a cousin's wedding in Jaipur, Nani asks to see last Diwali's photos. They were taken on your old phone, months ago.

What we are building

We are building access to saved photos through the phone app. Your phone and home device will both need internet access when you are away.
