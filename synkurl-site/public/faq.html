The product

What is Synkurl?

Synkurl is building a small device for your home and an app for your phone. The aim is to automatically save an extra copy of your photos and videos at home, then let you view those saved copies through the app. The first version is still in development.

What does backup mean?

A backup is an extra copy. The plan is to copy photos to the home device. Making a copy does not free space on your phone by itself. We have not confirmed how deleting photos from a phone or the device will work.

Do the six designs have different features?

They show different shapes and colours. We have not confirmed different storage sizes or speeds for them. Box Plus is a design name, not a promise of extra storage or performance.

Where will my photos be stored?

The planned photo storage is a device in your home. Using it away from home will need internet access. That does not mean the product runs without any online services; we will explain those services before early access.

Which phones will it work with?

We are starting with Android. iPhone support and its timing are not confirmed. You can still tell us which phone you use when you join the waitlist.

Do I need to be technical?

We are designing the setup for people who do not work with technology. We still need to test it with families. The aim is to connect the device, install the app and follow a few clear steps.

What if our home internet goes down?

You will not be able to reach the home device from elsewhere while its internet connection is down. Losing internet does not by itself erase photos already saved on its storage. How the app works inside the home during an outage still needs testing.

What if I lose my phone?

A copy that finished saving to the home device would not disappear just because the phone is lost. Getting those saved copies onto a replacement phone is part of what we are building. Photos that never finished copying would not be covered.

What if the device breaks?

Keeping photos at home does not protect them from every problem. If the storage drive fails and it holds the only copy, photos could be lost. A second-copy and recovery plan is still being worked out. We will explain it before early access.

How much can it store?

Storage options have not been announced. We will share more product details before early access.

When can I get it?

We are building and testing the first version. A release date and price have not been announced. Joining the waitlist means asking for an early-access email; it is not a purchase or a guarantee of a place.

The waitlist

What do I get by joining?

An email when early access opens, before we tell anyone else. Nothing else: no newsletters, no ads.

How are my details used?

Only to tell you about early access, and to plan which phones and places to support first. We don't sell them or use them for advertising. Read the privacy notice.

Why do you ask for my phone, city and family size?

Your phone tells us which phones to support first. Your city helps us plan where to start. Your family size tells us how many people's photos a family wants to keep together. If you tell us which design you'd put in your home, that helps us shape the device.

I didn't get the email.

Follow the message shown after joining. If it says you are on the waitlist, there may be no confirmation email. If it asks you to check your email, look in Promotions and Spam too. Still nothing? Try the form again after 10 minutes, or write to synkurl@synkurl.com.

Can I join from outside India?

Yes, anyone can join. We're starting in India, and the waitlist helps us decide where to go next.

How do I get removed?

Before you confirm, use the "Not me: remove my email" link in our email. Any time after that, write to synkurl@synkurl.com from the address you joined with. We'll delete your details and reply to confirm.
